Support

We’re here to help.

Little Things is a small, private app made with care. If something isn’t working, or you just have a question, the fastest way to reach a real person is email.

Email support↗

Contact

Email kris@singhstudio.co.nz. We read every message. There’s no account and no login — just tell us your device (iPhone or Android) and what you’re seeing.

Your data

Where it lives

Everything you write stays on your device. There’s no account and no Little Things cloud copy of your people or notes.

Export a copy

In the app, you can export everything as a plain JSON file at any time. It’s a point-in-time snapshot — store it somewhere private.

Delete your data

In the app, Settings → Clear all data removes the people, notes, glints, chronicles, and settings stored on your device. To ask us to delete any crash reports or analytics associated with your install, email kris@singhstudio.co.nz.

Little Things Full

Little Things is free for up to eight people. Little Things Full is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited people, custom labels, saved views, and the curated themes. It’s never a subscription.

Restore a purchase

If you reinstall or move to a new device on the same store account, open the upgrade sheet (Settings → Little Things Full, or by adding a ninth person) and choose Restore to bring your purchase back at no charge.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the App Store and Google Play. Refund requests go through Apple or Google directly, under their refund policies.

Common questions

Reminders aren’t showing up

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device. Make sure notifications are allowed for Little Things in your phone’s system settings, and that the person still has the date or nudge set.

I lost my data after reinstalling

Because Little Things is local-first with no cloud sync, deleting the app removes its on-device data. Export regularly if you keep meaningful notes — restore from an export is manual and replace-only.

Is the app lock encryption?

No. The biometric/passcode lock is a barrier on the app’s screen, not database encryption. See the privacy policy for the full, plain detail.
