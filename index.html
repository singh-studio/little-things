What this is

Caring about people is not the same as managing them.

Little Things is a private memory app for your close relationships. Add someone you care about, save the details you want to remember, and set gentle reminders for moments that matter. It is not a CRM, a productivity system, or a social network, just a simple place for the human details that usually end up scattered across your notes app, calendar, messages, or memory.

Privacy

Private by default. Honest about the limits.

Little Things is for notes you would not want sitting in someone else’s cloud.

The people you add, the details you save, the moments you keep, and the reminders you set all live on your device. Little Things does not keep a cloud copy, and we cannot read what you write.

The only exception is beta feedback, and that only leaves your device when you choose to send it.

01

No account.

You can use Little Things without signing in.

There is no profile to manage, no login to remember, and no account for us to recover.

02

No Little Things cloud.

Your relationship notes are stored locally on your device.

We do not back them up, sync them, or read them.

03

Local reminders.

Reminders are handled by your device, not sent from our server.

That means they can work offline, and your reminders do not require Little Things to keep a cloud copy of your data.

04

Plain export.

You can export your data as a JSON file.

It is there so you can keep your own copy, but treat it carefully. It is plain text, not encrypted.

What we’re honest about.

Little Things gives you privacy by keeping your data local, not by making big security promises.

App lock is for casual privacy. It can help stop someone from opening the app casually, but it is not database encryption.

Beta feedback is separate and opt-in. When you tap send, the message you wrote and limited app context are sent to the Little Things team so we can understand what happened and improve the app.

These limits are explained in the privacy policy and data promises.

Still wondering

Four questions we still get asked.

Who is Little Things actually for?+

People who want to remember birthdays, preferences, gift ideas, life updates, and the small things friends, family, partners, or a close circle mention in passing. Personal memory software, not a work tool.

How does the beta work?+

Register interest, get an invite when a spot opens, try it with a small circle, and send feedback when something feels useful, unclear, or too much. The tester group is intentionally small.

What does export actually give me?+

A point-in-time JSON file with everything you’ve added. It is plain text — not sync, not restore, not encrypted. Treat it like a sensitive notebook and keep it somewhere safe.

Is this a CRM?+

No. No pipelines, no contact scores, no follow-up quotas. Little Things is shaped for personal memory, not for managing relationships like accounts.
