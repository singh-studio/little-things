About the app

Caring about people is not the same as managing them.

Little Things is a private memory app for your close relationships. Add someone, save what matters, and set gentle reminders for moments when a message would help. It’s not a CRM, a productivity system, or a social network. It’s where the birthday, coffee order, gift idea, and hard week can live without turning relationships into tasks.

Privacy

Private by default. Honest about the limits.

Little Things is for personal notes that should stay personal.

The people you add, the details you save, the moments you keep, and the reminders you set all live on your device. Little Things doesn’t keep a cloud copy, and the Little Things team can’t read what you write.

Beta feedback is the exception. It leaves your device only when you choose to send it.

01

No account.

You can use Little Things without signing in.

There’s no profile to manage, no login to remember, and no account recovery.

02

No Little Things cloud.

Your relationship notes are stored locally on your device.

Little Things doesn’t back them up, sync them, or read them.

03

Local reminders.

Reminders are handled by your device, not sent from a server.

That means they can work offline, and your reminders don’t require Little Things to keep a cloud copy of your data.

04

Plain export.

You can export your data as a JSON file.

It’s there so you can keep your own copy, but treat it carefully. It’s plain text, not encrypted.

Worth knowing.

Local storage is the privacy model. App lock helps with casual privacy, but it isn’t database encryption. Exported JSON is plain text. Beta feedback is separate and opt-in.

More detail is in the privacy policy and data promises.

Still wondering

A few practical questions.

Who is Little Things for?+

People who want to remember birthdays, preferences, gift ideas, life updates, and the small things friends, family, partners, or a close circle mention in passing. It’s for personal memory, not work.

How does the beta work?+

Register interest, get an invite when a spot opens, try the beta with a small circle, and send feedback when something helps, confuses you, or gets in the way. The tester group is intentionally small.

What does export actually give me?+

A point-in-time JSON file with everything you’ve added. It’s plain text — not sync, not restore, not encrypted. Treat it like a sensitive notebook and keep it somewhere safe.

Is this a CRM?+

No. No pipelines, no contact scores, no follow-up quotas. Little Things is shaped for personal memory, not for managing relationships like accounts.
