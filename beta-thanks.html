What happens next

Kris will review your request and check whether this beta is a good fit right now.

If you are added to a TestFlight group, Apple will send the official TestFlight email with the install link.

There is nothing else to do for now.

A few honest beta notes

  • There is no account and no Little Things cloud.
  • Your relationship notes live on your device.
  • Export is the current backup path.
  • Beta builds may still change, break, or need a reinstall.
  • Feedback only leaves your device when you choose to send it.

Before you install

The beta is best for people who are comfortable trying an early app and giving clear feedback when something feels confusing, broken, too much, or genuinely useful.

You can read the privacy policy, data promises, and beta FAQ while your request is being reviewed.
