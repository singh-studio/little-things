Terms for a personal app.

Little Things is a personal memory app for close relationships. These terms explain what the app is, what it isn’t, and where the limits are.

AboutBetaYour contentSecurityUse it wellLimits

What Little Things is

Little Things helps you remember small details about people you care about and set gentle reminders for moments that matter. Data you enter is stored locally on your device. There’s no account system, no cloud sync, and no server that holds your relationship data.

Beta means still evolving

  • Features may change or behave differently across updates.
  • Little Things may have bugs that affect stored data.
  • Terms may change as the product matures.

If you’re using TestFlight or early access, treat this as an early product. Export data you care about.

Your content is yours

You’re responsible for the names, notes, gift ideas, chronicles, and other information you type or import. Little Things stores that data locally. You own it. Little Things doesn’t own it, access it, or back it up.

Device security matters

Your device is your vault. Use a device passcode. Keep your device physically secure. Don’t leave the app open on a shared or unlocked device.

The biometric lock in Little Things is a UI access barrier, not database encryption. Exported files are plain text and aren’t encrypted.

Use it for genuine relationships

Little Things is designed for one person’s private use on their own device. Don’t use it in ways that pressure or harm someone.

No guarantee of recovery

Little Things is provided as-is. It doesn’t guarantee data recovery after device loss, clear-all-data, reinstall, or a failed update. Export is your best safeguard during beta.
