Terms for a personal app.

Little Things is a personal relationship notes app. These terms are written plainly so beta users know what the app is, what it is not, and where the limits are.

What it isBetaYour contentSecurityUse it wellLimits

What Little Things is

Little Things helps you keep track of people, remember things about them, and stay in touch. Data you enter is stored locally on your device. There is no account system, no cloud sync, and no server that holds your relationship data.

Beta means still evolving

  • Features may change or behave differently across updates.
  • The app may have bugs that affect stored data.
  • Terms may change as the product matures.

If you are using TestFlight or early access, treat this as an early product. Export data you care about.

Your content is yours

You are responsible for the names, notes, gift ideas, chronicles, and other information you type or import. The app stores that data locally. You own it. Little Things does not own it, access it, or back it up.

Device security matters

Your device is your vault. Use a device passcode. Keep your device physically secure. Do not leave the app open on a shared or unlocked device.

The biometric lock in Little Things is a UI access barrier, not database encryption. Exported files are plain text and are not encrypted.

Use it for genuine relationships

Little Things is designed for one person's private use on their own device. Do not use it to stalk, surveil, harm, or track someone in a context where you do not have a personal relationship with them.

No guarantee of recovery

Little Things is provided as-is. It does not guarantee data recovery after device loss, clear-all-data, reinstall, or a failed update. Export is your best safeguard during beta.
