What happens to your data.

What stays on your device, what can leave it, and what the beta doesn’t promise.

Data typesExportDeleteRetainWhat isn’t promised

Three kinds of data

Relationship data

People, circles, glints, gift ideas, chronicles, birthdays, profile photos, settings, and preferences.

Beta feedback

Feedback text you choose to send, plus limited app context like screen, build, platform, and optional rating.

Beta analytics

Basic usage data such as screen views, feature interactions, session length, build version, and counts grouped into ranges.

Export promises

  • Export creates a point-in-time JSON snapshot.
  • Export is the current way to get relationship data out of the app.
  • Exported files are plain text and not encrypted by Little Things.
  • Export is not sync, restore, or automatic backup.

Deletion promises

Clear all data deletes the local app data Little Things manages and attempts to cancel reminders tied to that data. This can’t be undone inside the app.

Clear all data doesn’t delete exported files, operating system backups, delivered notifications, or beta feedback you already submitted.

Retention promises

Relationship data stays local until you delete it, uninstall the app, lose or damage the device, or a beta or OS issue affects stored data.

Feedback is kept long enough to review, follow up, and decide what to fix or improve. Analytics is for product signal, not relationship content.

What Little Things doesn’t claim

  • Export is encrypted.
  • Export is restore.
  • Delete is reversible.
  • Little Things stores relationship data on a server.
  • Beta analytics is anonymous or collects personal relationship content.
